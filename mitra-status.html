What is still missing

Priority gaps before a bank deployment

1. Real data integration

  • IDBI core-banking and Account Aggregator connectivity
  • Broker, AMC, insurance, tax and loan data feeds
  • Live market and fund data instead of synthetic scenarios

2. Production platform

  • Secure server-side AI keys and calculation authority
  • Durable transactional database and scalable services
  • Monitoring, rate limits, recovery, retention and deletion controls

3. Regulated execution

  • Consent lifecycle and auditable customer permissions
  • Approved risk profiling and periodic review
  • Live SIP, MF, FD or RM workflows with human oversight
