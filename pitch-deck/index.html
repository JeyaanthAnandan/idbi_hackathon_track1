The Problem
01 — The Problem

Wealth advisory never
reached 98% of customers.

01

Fragmented & inaccessible

Human relationship managers can only serve the top ~2% of customers — everyone else gets no advisory relationship at all, however much they save.

02

Data sitting idle

The bank already holds salary credits, spends, deposits and SIP behaviour for every account — but none of it is converted into advice today.

03

Generic, not personal

What customers get instead is generic product pushes — not the timely, personalized, data-driven guidance an RM would give an HNI client.

In our own synthetic profile for an average salaried IDBI customer, ₹25,125 sits idle every single month — earning savings-account interest instead of compounding — simply because nobody ever said anything.

Why MITRA Wins
02 — Approach & Differentiators

Not a chatbot bolted on.
An advisory engine, worn as a friend.

Our approach: compute real advice deterministically from behavioural data first, use conversational AI only to phrase it — so every recommendation is explainable, auditable, and free to deliver at scale.

Fully offline, demo-proof

Onboarding → dashboard → nudge → avatar voice conversation → chart → executed SIP, end-to-end. Nothing on stage can break.

Computed, not canned

Every score, nudge, projection and recommendation is derived live from data. Change one input, and everything downstream changes with it.

Hybrid AI a regulated bank can ship

Deterministic, auditable advisory engine for the numbers; optional LLM only for open-ended phrasing, grounded in that same computed context.

Scales to every customer

Near-zero marginal cost per customer, in their own language — democratizing what an RM does today only for HNIs.

USP The only wealth advisor priced at zero marginal cost per customer — because every recommendation comes from data the bank already has, not a headcount the bank has to hire.

Process Flow
04 — How It Works

Data in. Explainable action out.

One continuous loop — from raw transaction data to a one-tap action the customer actually takes.

Behavioural Data

Salary credits · spends · deposits · SIPs & holdings, 6 months back.

Analytics Engine

Surplus & anomaly detection · health score · drift · 80C gap. Deterministic & auditable.

Conversational Engine

17 intents · English + हिंदी · avatar voice · optional LLM phrasing, grounded in the data above.

Explainable Advice

Every recommendation ships with "🔍 Why this advice?" — SEBI-suitability aligned.

One-tap Action

Start SIP · Enable Round-Up · Approve glide path — inside the bank app.

Every executed action becomes new behavioural data — the loop closes and re-scores automatically.
